# Huishouden

Simple shared apps for running a home together, on everyone's phone and on a tablet in the
kitchen. *Huishouden* is Dutch for "household".

**Open it:** [huishouden-piekstra.web.app](https://huishouden-piekstra.web.app): one site, installed once,
with every app inside it. Free for any household: sign in with Google, start a household, invite the
people you live with. In English, Spanish and Dutch, light or dark.

## The apps

| App | |
|---|---|
| [Huishouden](https://github.com/huishouden/portal) | Today, the calendar and the to-do list across every app, in one place |
| [Tasks](https://github.com/huishouden/tasks) | Shared to-dos and chores |
| [Groceries](https://github.com/huishouden/groceries) | What to get, and where it is |
| [Home](https://github.com/huishouden/home) | Keeping the house in good shape |
| [Bills](https://github.com/huishouden/bills) | What's due, and what's paid |
| [Spending](https://github.com/huishouden/spending) | Where the household's money goes |
| [Pet](https://github.com/huishouden/pet) | Caring for the animals in the family |
| [Baby](https://github.com/huishouden/baby) | Looking after the little one, together |
| [Health](https://github.com/huishouden/health) | Medicines and care for everyone at home |
| [Car](https://github.com/huishouden/car) | Keeping the cars on the road |

## Beyond the apps

| | |
|---|---|
| [Connector](https://github.com/huishouden/connector) | Use Huishouden from your own AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini CLI) |
| [Calendar](https://github.com/huishouden/calendar) | Your household in your own calendar, and card alerts read from your inbox |
| [Notify](https://github.com/huishouden/notify) | Reminders as notifications on your devices |

## Building it

| | |
|---|---|
| [Kit](https://github.com/huishouden/pwa-kit) | The shared building blocks every app is made from, so they look and work alike |
| [Rules](https://github.com/huishouden/rules) | Who in a household may see and change what |

Each household's data stays in its own space, readable only by its members and only as their role
allows; helpers and kids see less. See the [privacy page](https://huishouden-piekstra.web.app/privacy).
